# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** January 2026

Task Tracker (“the app”) is a productivity application developed and published by an individual developer. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles user data.

---

## 1. Data Collection

Task Tracker does **not collect, store, or share any personal data**.

- The app does **not** require user accounts or authentication.
- The app does **not** collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or identifiers.
- The app does **not** collect location data.
- The app does **not** use analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party trackers.

---

## 2. Data Storage

All task data is stored **locally on your device only**.

- Tasks, reminders, and settings remain on your device.
- No data is transmitted to external servers.
- The developer does not have access to your data.

---

## 3. Permissions

The app may request the following permissions:

- **Notifications** – Used only to remind you about tasks you have created.
- **Exact alarm / background scheduling (if applicable)** – Used solely for task reminders.

These permissions are used **only for app functionality** and not for data collection or tracking.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

Task Tracker does **not** use third-party services that collect user data, including:

- Advertising networks
- Analytics services
- Cloud storage providers

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy

Task Tracker does not knowingly collect any personal data from children under the age of 13. Since the app does not collect any user data, it is safe for general audiences.

---

## 6. Data Security

Because all data remains on your device and is not transmitted externally, the risk of data exposure is minimal. Users are responsible for securing their own devices.

---

## 7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
